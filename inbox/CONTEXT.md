# inbox - the current brief

One job: hold the brief for the run that is in progress. One brief at a time.

## Inputs
- Reference (every run): `../_templates/brief.md`

Do NOT load: stage outputs, `_shared/ladder.md`, `_shared/sources.md`.

## Process
1. Copy `_templates/brief.md` to `inbox/brief.md`.
2. Fill the brief. One topic. Leave the template unchanged.

## Outputs
- `brief.md` in this folder

## Check
A person confirms the brief names the reader, the purpose, and the audience before stage 01 starts.
