## Standards

- ASD-STE100 Simplified Technical English, Issue 9 (2025-01-15). ASD owns the copyright and the trademark. The official PDF is free to request from <https://www.asd-ste100.org/>. The agent digest is [ste-issue-9.md](../stages/01_doc-clarity/references/ste-issue-9.md). The checker word list is [ste-rules.md](../stages/01_doc-clarity/references/ste-rules.md). Neither file is the official dictionary. Certify only against the official issue.
- ISO 24495-1:2023, Plain language, Part 1: Governing principles and guidelines. Record: <https://www.iso.org/standard/78907.html>. The four principles in the distillation are relevant, findable, understandable, and usable.
